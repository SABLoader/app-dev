# My Favorite Series: **Stranger Things**

## Overview
*Stranger Things* is a thrilling sci-fi series on Netflix that combines mystery, horror, and 80s nostalgia.  

## Main Characters
- **Eleven** – A girl with telekinetic powers  
- **Mike** – Eleven's loyal friend  
- **Dustin** – The funny and clever member of the group  
- **Will** – The boy who disappears into the Upside Down  

## Why I Like It
1. Exciting and suspenseful plot twists  
2. Strong friendships and character development  
3. Nostalgic 80s references and music  

## Fun Fact
The series was created by the Duffer Brothers and first premiered in 2016.
